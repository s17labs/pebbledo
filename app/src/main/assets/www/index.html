Welcome to
PebbleDo

A quiet, minimal to-do list.
No accounts. No syncing. Just you and your tasks.

Write anything

Tap any row to start typing. Press ↵ Enter to create the next task. Long tasks wrap automatically.

Check it off

Tap the circle on a task to archive it. It crosses out and fades away. Tap to see everything archived.

Reorder freely

Hold and drag the grip on any task to move it to a new position.

Make it yours

Tap to pick a theme, font, size, and corner radius. Everything saves automatically.
